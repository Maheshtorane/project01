Home, someday

And someday, when our hair has silvered and our steps have grown a little slower, I imagine a tiny home resting on a hill, with flowers outside the window and warm yellow lights glowing through the evening. But I hope we'll look at each other and find the same warmth we found at the very beginning. Because if there's one place I'd choose to arrive at after every road, every season, and every version of ourselves, it's a life where we still get to make each other smile.
